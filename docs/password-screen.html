32-team passwords

Version 1 uses the game's existing 22-symbol screen. A password stores the seed team, completed matches, results and CPU ownership for every team.

Enter the symbols on the game keyboard, then choose END. A valid password opens the restored bracket. CONTINUE PLAY reopens that bracket, and LIST OF TEAMS opens a view that returns to the title with A or B.

The alphabet is 1AKV5EPR2BLW6FGS3CMX78HT4DNY09JU. The aliases I/1, O/0, Q/9 and Z/2 are accepted. CRC-16 and reserved-field checks reject mistyped or malformed passwords.

Stock 16-team championship passwords are not compatible. The previous hex, 16-letter and 11-symbol mockups are superseded.
