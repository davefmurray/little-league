JAPAN

Roster

15 slots · 6-char names · RGB nibbles 0–15
# Name Skill 1–5 Batting 0–15 Pitching 0–15 Speed 0–15

SKILL is the card rating (byte 11). BATTING and PITCHING are the two nibbles of byte 9. SPEED is the low nibble of byte 10, which the game copies into its baserunning variable.

A red P marks a pitcher slot. All other bytes are preserved from the source record — export only changes names, ratings, skill and the three stats, and is otherwise byte-identical to the loaded ROM.
