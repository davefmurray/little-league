The game loads players 1–15 for every team. These records stay in the file exactly as built, so they are shown here read-only.

# Name Skill Batting Pitching Speed

SKILL is the 1–5 rating on each player card. BATTING and PITCHING are two 0–15 ratings that share one byte. SPEED is the 0–15 value the game copies into its baserunning variable.

Roster Lab opens the original 16-team US ROM and the 32-team Preview 8 ROM, including copies it exported. Export writes only the names, skills, stats and team ratings you changed; every other byte is copied from your file. A red P marks a player linked to one of the team's three pitcher records, which this editor does not change.

Source and data map: github.com/davefmurray/little-league. The full game write-up covers its history from Koushien to the Little League World Series.
