What changes in the ROM

Preview 8 shares one color record between each team and the team 16 places later. The first color export changes 3 bytes in the game's color routine and fills 96 unused bytes with a record for every team: 99 bytes in all. Later color edits change only their own bytes, and roster-only exports stay as they are.

About Roster Lab

Skill is the 1–5 rating on each player card. Bat and Pitch are two 0–15 ratings that share one byte. Speed is the 0–15 value the game copies into its baserunning variable. The uniform colors are the three NES color codes the game loads for a team's cap and trim, jersey and hair.

Roster Lab opens the original 16-team US ROM and the 32-team Preview 8 ROM, including copies it exported. Download ROM saves the names, skills, stats, team ratings and team colors you changed. The first Preview 8 color edit also adds independent color records for all 32 teams; every other byte is copied from your file. A red P marks a player linked to one of the team's three pitcher records, which this editor does not change.

Source and data map: github.com/davefmurray/little-league. The full game write-up covers its history from Koushien to the Little League World Series.
